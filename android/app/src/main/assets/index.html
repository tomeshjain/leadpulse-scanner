How Wispr Flow Replaces Mobile Typing

Wispr Flow provides two universal mechanisms to eliminate typing across your phone:

1
Floating Flow HUD / Bubble Overlay

A floating microphone bubble hovers over WhatsApp, Slack, and Gmail. Tap it to speak, and your polished thoughts are auto-copied to the clipboard with instant paste.

2
Android Custom Keyboard (IME) Extension

In Android Settings > System > Languages > On-screen keyboard, enable Wispr Flow Keyboard. The AI Mic is built right into your spacebar for 1-tap text field injection.
